Privacy notice

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

Euro2Rupee (euro2rupee.net) is a personal community project run by Pavan Koppula in Ireland. For anything about your data, email pavan31.koppula@gmail.com.

What we collect

You can use the currency, sports, events and weather sections without an account, and we collect no personal data for them.

If you create an account for My Reminders, we store:

  • Account details: your email address and, if you give one, your name. Your password is never stored in readable form. Our login provider keeps only a secure hash of it.
  • Your reminders: what you enter, such as the title, category, due date, amount, repeat setting and notes.
  • Settings: your default reminder window.
  • Basic account records: when you signed up and last logged in.

Don't put sensitive information such as card numbers, PINs or passwords in reminder notes.

Why we use it

Only to run the service you signed up for: logging you in and showing you your reminders. The legal basis is performing our agreement with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We don't sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking.

Where it is stored

We use Google Firebase. Your profile and reminders are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU (Belgium, europe-west1). Logins are handled by Firebase Authentication, which runs on Google's global infrastructure and may process data outside the EU. Google provides this under its data processing terms, which include safeguards for international transfers such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

The currency section asks a public exchange-rate service (Frankfurter) for rates from your browser. Like any website, that service can see your IP address. No account information is sent to it.

Who can see it

Other users can never see your data. As the site operator, I can technically access stored account details and reminders through the Firebase console. I'll only do that when needed to run or fix the service, or when you ask me to, and never share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. Passwords can't be seen by anyone.

Storage on your device

When you log in, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay logged in, and the app keeps an offline copy of your reminders. These are needed for the service to work. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Signing out ends the session on that device.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it from My profile → Delete my account immediately removes your account, profile and all reminders. Google may keep encrypted backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: My profile → Download my data gives you a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Correction: edit your name and reminders at any time.
  • Deletion: My profile → Delete my account, or email us.
  • Objection or other requests: email us and we'll respond within one month.

You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Children

Accounts are not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be shown on the site.
